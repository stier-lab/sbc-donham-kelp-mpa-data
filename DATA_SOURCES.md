## 1. SBC MBON integrated kelp-forest datasets

These are the largest input files in the pipeline. They are **harmonized synthesis datasets** that pool field data from PISCO, KFM, LTER, and USGS San Nicolas Island monitoring programs into one schema. Each row is one diver-recorded observation of one taxon in one survey unit.

### 1.1 Quad and swath cover (2.7 M rows, 402 MB)

- **File:** `data/MBON/SBCMBON_kelp_forest_integrated_quad_and_swath_20231022.csv`
- **EDI package:** `edi.6.3` (or newer revision matching the 2023-10-22 file stamp)
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.6073/pasta/85a8f46dabe413973195c8530911088a
- **EDI metadata viewer:** https://portal.edirepository.org/nis/metadataviewer?packageid=edi.6.3
- **Citation:**
  > Miller, R.J., Rassweiler, A.R., Caselle, J.E., Kushner, D., Reed, D.C., Lafferty, K.D., Kui, L., & O'Brien, M. (2021). *Santa Barbara Channel Marine BON: Nearshore kelp forest integrated quad and swath cover, 1980–ongoing* (ver. 6.3). Environmental Data Initiative. https://doi.org/10.6073/pasta/85a8f46dabe413973195c8530911088a
- **Coverage:** 1980–present, ~100 sites in the Santa Barbara Channel and Channel Islands.
- **Schema:** `data_source` (kfm / pisco / lter / sni), `sample_method` (1mquad / 5mquad / 10mquad / swath), `date`, `site_id`, `subsite_id`, `transect_id`, `replicate_id`, `proj_taxon_id`, `area`, `count`, `auth_taxon_id`, `auth_name`, `taxon_name`, `site_name`, `subsite_name`, `latitude`, `longitude`.
- **How we use it:** Pipeline subsets to `data_source == "kfm"` and the five focal taxa (urchins, Macrocystis, lobster). The `area` column is taken at face value. For KFM Macrocystis we filter to `area == 10` (the modern paired-diver 5 m × 1 m × 2-divers protocol). See `05_kfm_processing.R::Section 11`.

### 1.2 Fish (4.8 M rows, 780 MB)

- **File:** `data/MBON/SBCMBON_kelp_forest_integrated_fish_20231022.csv`
- **EDI package:** `edi.5.3`
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.6073/pasta/0976f9969ef2789b77b659e9337c4c0c
- **EDI metadata viewer:** https://portal.edirepository.org/nis/metadataviewer?packageid=edi.5.3
- **Citation:**
  > Miller, R.J., Rassweiler, A.R., Caselle, J.E., Kushner, D., Reed, D.C., Lafferty, K.D., Kui, L., & O'Brien, M. (2018). *Santa Barbara Channel Marine BON: Nearshore kelp forest integrated fish, 1981–ongoing* (ver. 5.3). Environmental Data Initiative. https://doi.org/10.6073/pasta/0976f9969ef2789b77b659e9337c4c0c
- **Coverage:** 1981–present.
- **Schema:** `data_source`, `sample_method`, `sample_subtype`, `date`, `site_id`, `subsite_id`, `transect_id`, `replicate_id`, `proj_taxon_id`, `area`, `height`, `count`, `auth_taxon_id`, `auth_name`, `taxon_name`, `site_name`, `subsite_name`, `latitude`, `longitude`.
- **How we use it:** Pipeline subsets to `data_source == "kfm"` and KFM-specific sheephead taxon IDs (`t-k-127` = visual fish; `t-k-233` = roving diver fish count) for the KFM sheephead density-only contribution. See `05_kfm_processing.R::Section 13`.

### 1.3 Site geolocation (companion table, 65 rows)

- **File:** `data/MBON/SBCMBON_kelp_forest_site_geolocation_20210120_KFM_LTER.csv`
- **Provenance:** This is one of five auxiliary tables shipped inside `edi.5.3` (the integrated fish package). Same citation as 1.2.
- **Schema:** `site_id`, `geolocation`, `data_source`, `Lat`, `Lon`, `status` (mpa / reference), `CA_MPA_Name_Short`, `ChannelIsland`, `MPA_Start`.
- **How we use it:** Joins MBON site IDs to our internal `CA_MPA_Name_Short` keys and provides MPA implementation dates for the Before/After assignment. Used as `Sites2` in `05_kfm_processing.R`.

## 2. SBC LTER kelp-forest direct datasets

These are the LTER's own tabular exports, distinct from the MBON harmonization. We use them for LTER-only taxa (LTER Macrocystis, LTER fish for sheephead, LTER lobsters).

### 2.1 LTER kelp (Macrocystis frond counts)

- **File:** `data/LTER/Annual_Kelp_All_Years_20240305.csv` (29,527 rows)
- **EDI package:** `knb-lter-sbc.18.29` (or newer revision)
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.6073/pasta/9bdb2dcaece1f4ab933b54f7b8b68144
- **EDI metadata viewer:** https://portal.edirepository.org/nis/metadataviewer?packageid=knb-lter-sbc.18.29
- **Citation:**
  > Reed, D.C. & R.J. Miller. 2024. *SBC LTER: Reef: Kelp Forest Community Dynamics: Abundance and size of Giant Kelp (Macrocystis pyrifera), ongoing since 2000.* Environmental Data Initiative. https://doi.org/10.6073/pasta/9bdb2dcaece1f4ab933b54f7b8b68144
- **Schema:** `YEAR`, `MONTH`, `DATE`, `SITE`, `TRANSECT`, `QUAD`, `SIDE`, `SP_CODE`, `FRONDS`, `HLD_DIAM`, `AREA` (20 or 40 m²), `OBS_CODE`, `NOTES`, `SCIENTIFIC_NAME`, `COMMON_NAME`, taxonomy fields.
- **FRONDS column** is operationally a stipe count: per the SBC LTER kelp protocol PDF (https://sbclter.msi.ucsb.edu/external/Reef/Protocols/Kelp_Forest_Community_Dynamics/SBC_LTER_protocol_Reed_Kelp_forest_community_Density_giant_kelp_20130524.pdf), a frond is *"any blade > 10 cm long that is still attached to the stipe"* counted at 1 m above the holdfast.
- **How we use it:** Density = FRONDS / AREA per row, then convert via `bio_macro()`. See `06_lter_processing.R::Section 5`.

### 2.2 LTER fish

- **File:** `data/LTER/Annual_fish_comb_20240307.csv` (93,811 data rows; header excluded)
- **EDI package:** `knb-lter-sbc.17.39` (published 2024-03-07)
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.6073/pasta/6ea061f660a7dc939686f51613b5ff0e
- **EDI metadata viewer:** https://portal.edirepository.org/nis/metadataviewer?packageid=knb-lter-sbc.17.39
- **Exact-release EML:** https://pasta.lternet.edu/package/metadata/eml/knb-lter-sbc/17/39; retrieved via [DataONE resolve](https://cn.dataone.org/cn/v2/resolve/https%3A%2F%2Fpasta.lternet.edu%2Fpackage%2Fmetadata%2Feml%2Fknb-lter-sbc%2F17%2F39) on 2026-10-10 after direct PASTA access was denied.
- **Release verification:** EML `physical/objectName`, `numberOfRecords` and MD5 authentication match the local file: `Annual_fish_comb_20240307.csv`, 93,811 and `ddf0cb97132a35aede43a8057709ffee`. The EML DOI alternate identifier and [EDI citation service](https://cite.edirepository.org/cite/knb-lter-sbc.17.39?style=RAW) both identify the DOI above and version 39.
- **Citation:**
  > Reed, D.C. & R.J. Miller. 2024. *SBC LTER: Reef: Kelp Forest Community Dynamics: Fish abundance* (ver. 39). Environmental Data Initiative. https://doi.org/10.6073/pasta/6ea061f660a7dc939686f51613b5ff0e
- **Schema:** `YEAR`, `MONTH`, `DATE`, `SITE`, `TRANSECT`, `QUAD`, `SIDE`, `VIS`, `SP_CODE`, `SIZE`, `COUNT`, `AREA` (20 or 80 m², occasional 40), taxonomy fields.
- **COUNT semantics** (exact-release EML verified 2026-10-10): COUNT is the number of individuals sharing the recorded SIZE, not one individual per row. SIZE is estimated individual size in cm; `-99999` denotes unavailable/not-recorded COUNT or SIZE. No new provider communication is asserted by this metadata verification.
- **AREA conventions** (verified empirically 2026-05-04): 80 m² for "BIG" mobile fish (sheephead and other large-bodied species; 60,304 obs); 20 m² for "CRYPTIC" small benthic fish (gobies, blennies, sculpins; 32,877 obs); 40 m² for an intermediate subset (630 obs). Sheephead (SP_CODE = SPUL) is uniformly on the 80 m² protocol.
- **How we use it:** Filter to `SCIENTIFIC_NAME == "Semicossyphus pulcher"`, defensive AREA check, then row biomass = `COUNT * bio_sheephead(SIZE)` before existing transect/site/MPA aggregation; density continues to use COUNT. COUNT weighting was corrected on 2026-10-10. See `06_lter_processing.R::Section 6` and the focused, dry-run-first `export_lter_sheephead_count.R` driver.

### 2.3 LTER lobster

- **File:** `data/LTER/Lobster_Abundance_All_Years_20230831.csv` (8,342 rows)
- **EDI package:** `knb-lter-sbc.77.10`
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.6073/pasta/63eca8e267cdaf016709e5c1b3d746d4
- **EDI metadata viewer:** https://portal.edirepository.org/nis/metadataviewer?packageid=knb-lter-sbc.77.10
- **Citation:**
  > Reed, D.C. & R.J. Miller. 2024. *SBC LTER: Reef: Abundance, size and fishing effort for California Spiny Lobster (Panulirus interruptus), ongoing since 2012.* Environmental Data Initiative. https://doi.org/10.6073/pasta/63eca8e267cdaf016709e5c1b3d746d4
- **Schema:** `YEAR`, `MONTH`, `DATE`, `SITE`, `TRANSECT`, `REPLICATE`, `SIZE_MM`, `COUNT`, `NUM_AO`, `AREA` (uniformly 300 m² = 60 m × 5 m benthic plot).
- **Coverage:** 2012–2023, five sites (two MPA: Naples, Isla Vista; three reference: Arroyo Quemado, Mohawk, Carpinteria).
- **How we use it:** Density = COUNT / AREA, biomass = `bio_lobster(SIZE_MM)` × COUNT. See `06_lter_processing.R::Section 4`.

## 3. PISCO kelp-forest community datasets

These are the PISCO subtidal community surveys (Partnership for Interdisciplinary Studies of Coastal Oceans). Two research groups contribute: UCSB (since 1999) and VRG (Vantuna Research Group, Occidental College, since 2003).

### 3.1 PISCO swath (kelp + invertebrates)

- **File:** `data/PISCO/MLPA_kelpforest_swath_2024.csv` (329,197 rows; ~39 MB)
- **Data deposit DOI:** https://doi.org/10.6085/AA/PISCO_kelpforest.1.6 (PISCO Metacat / KNB)
- **Data deposit landing:** https://data.piscoweb.org/metacatui/#view/doi:10.6085/AA/PISCO_kelpforest.1.6
- **Data paper citation:**
  > Malone, D.P., Davis, K., Lonhart, S.I., Parsons-Field, A., Caselle, J.E., & Carr, M.H. (2022). Large-scale, multidecade monitoring data from kelp forest ecosystems in California and Oregon (USA). *Ecology* 103(5): e3630. https://doi.org/10.1002/ecy.3630
- **Schema:** `campus` (UCSB / VRG), `method`, `survey_year`, `year`, `month`, `day`, `site`, `zone`, `transect`, `classcode`, `count`, `size`, `disease`, `depth`, `observer`, `notes`, `site_name_old`.
- **Transect dimensions:** 30 m × 2 m = 60 m² (no AREA column; implied by protocol. See Caselle et al. 2015 *Sci Rep* and Eisaguirre et al. 2020 *Ecology*).
- **Version note:** The 2024 file refresh is more recent than the v1.6 archived deposit. Cite Malone et al. 2022 plus the Metacat DOI. In our Methods note that we use a 2024 PISCO data pull.
- **How we use it:** All five focal taxa for PISCO's contribution. Lobster sizing is direct (UCSB) or via roving-diver bootstrap (VRG). See `04_pisco_processing.R`.

### 3.2 PISCO fish

- **File:** `data/PISCO/MLPA_kelpforest_fish_2024.csv` (517,113 rows; ~80 MB)
- **Same data deposit + citation as 3.1.**
- **Schema:** `campus`, `method`, `survey_year`, `year`, `month`, `day`, `site`, `zone`, `level` (BOT / MID / CNMD / CAN), `transect`, `classcode`, `count`, `fish_tl`, `min_tl`, `max_tl`, `sex`, `observer`, `depth`, `vis`, `temp`, `surge`, `pctcnpy`, `notes`, `site_name_old`, `LOOKEDFOR`.
- **Vertical-level convention:** BOT (bottom, on/near seafloor); MID (mid-water, ~halfway up); CNMD (canopy-mid; used at sites where kelp canopy is too shallow for a separate canopy diver); CAN (canopy). Reference: Rassweiler et al. 2020 *Front Mar Sci*, https://doi.org/10.3389/fmars.2020.00272.
- **How we use it:** Filter to sheephead (SPUL) and to BOT/MID/CNMD (excluding CAN). Toggle `SHEEPHEAD_BOT_ONLY` is available for BOT-only sensitivity. See `04_pisco_processing.R::Section 9`.

### 3.3 PISCO master site table (Emily-edited)

- **File:** `data/PISCO/master_site_table_Emilyedit.csv` (306 rows)
- **Provenance:** PISCO's standard `master_site_table` augmented by Emily Donham with site-MPA crosswalk corrections needed for this analysis.
- **Schema:** `LTM_project`, `campus`, `survey_year`, `site`, `site_new`, `latitude`, `longitude`, `CA_MPA_Name_Short`, `site_designation`, `site_status` (mpa / reference), `secondary_mpa`, `secondary_status`, `BaselineRegion`, `LongTermRegion`, `time_series_category`, `site_name_old`.
- **Caveat:** A small number of MPAs share the same reference site (one reference paired with two MPA sites). Documented in `04_pisco_processing.R` and accommodated by random effects in the joint meta-analysis.
- **Data-paper basis:** Malone et al. 2022 *Ecology* 103: e3630 (the master_site_table is shipped as part of the PISCO kelp-forest deposit).

### 3.4 PISCO species attribute table

- **File:** `data/PISCO/spp_attribute_table_downloaded_9-13-22_SHSPUL.csv` (330 rows; downloaded 2022-09-13)
- **Provenance:** PISCO species attribute lookup, distributed alongside the PISCO kelp-forest community data. Filename suffix `_SHSPUL` denotes a hand-tag added during this analysis (the species code for *Semicossyphus pulcher*, sheephead).
- **Same data-paper citation as 3.1** (Malone et al. 2022).
- **Schema:** `data_type`, `group`, `pisco_classcode`, `high_abundance`, `ecologically_significant`, ..., `WL_a`, `WL_b`, `LC.a._for_WL`, `LC.b._for_WL`, `WL_input_length`, `LC_type_for_WL`, `genus`, `species`, `common_name`, etc.
- **Coefficient sources:** Individual rows identify their lookup/publication sources. The original SPUL row contains 0.0238 and 2.8847 but names Williams et al. (2013), whose published standard-length equation is different. That exact numerical pair's source was not verified.
- **How we use it:** The original CSV is preserved (MD5 `6ccfacd18fff07208f510384e914a3b2`). `apply_sheephead_allometry()` overrides only the in-memory SPUL row with Hamilton & Caselle (2015), Methods p. 2: `W(g) = 0.0144 x TL(cm)^3.04`, DOI 10.1098/rspb.2014.1817. Both PISCO and LTER multiply individual mass by recorded size-class abundance before aggregation. Other fish retain their original lookup rows. The source/override record is exported as `pisco_sheephead_allometry_source.json`; the scoped promotion and current-input check use `sheephead_allometry.json`.

## 4. NPS Kelp Forest Monitoring (KFM): Channel Islands National Park

### 4.1 KFM Macrocystis raw stipe counts

- **File:** `data/MBON/KFM_Macrocystis_RawData_1984-2023.csv` (65,216 rows; 7.5 MB)
- **Originating program:** NPS Kelp Forest Monitoring (Channel Islands National Park).
- **Distribution:** Through the SBC MBON portal (file is in `data/MBON/`).
- **Authoritative protocol citation:**
  > Davis, G.E., Kushner, D.J., Mondragon, J.M., Mondragon, J.E., Lerma, D., & Richards, D.V. (1997). *Kelp Forest Monitoring Handbook, Volume 1: Sampling Protocol.* Channel Islands National Park, Ventura, CA. November 1997. 96 pp. https://irma.nps.gov/DataStore/DownloadFile/485444
- **Survey design (per Davis et al. 1997 p.27, p.28):** Divers count stipes on **exactly 100 individual giant-kelp plants per site per year**, sampled along the entire 100 m main transect with a swath width of 1–5 m (density-dependent). For each plant, the stipe count is taken **1 m above the bottom** and the maximum holdfast diameter is measured (cm). Filed under the protocol's Size Frequency table (`SizeFreqNatHab`).
- **Schema:** `SiteNumber`, `IslandCode`, `IslandName`, `SiteCode`, `SiteName`, `SurveyYear`, `SurveyDate`, `PermanentObserverNumber`, `Species`, `ScientificName`, `Marker`, `Stipe_Count`, `Diameter_cm`.
- **How we use it:** Bootstrap-resample stipe counts per site/year and convert via `bio_macro()` to estimate per-plant biomass. The KFM stipe data are the authoritative kelp biomass source for KFM sites. See `05_kfm_processing.R::Section 7`.

### 4.2 KFM internal urchin size-frequency (NOT YET INGESTED)

- **Available at:** NOAA ERDDAP `erdCinpKfmSFNH` (https://coastwatch.pfeg.noaa.gov/erddap/tabledap/erdCinpKfmSFNH). Coverage is 1985–2007 only. Post-2007 requires direct request from NPS Channel Islands KFM program.
- **Citation (same as KFM Macro stipe):** Davis et al. 1997, KFM Sampling Protocol Handbook.
- **Survey design (per Davis et al. 1997 p.26, p.28):** ~200 individuals per urchin species (*Strongylocentrotus purpuratus*, *Mesocentrotus franciscanus*) per site per year, max test diameter measured to nearest mm with stainless-steel calipers, since 1985.
- **Status:** Not currently in `ALL_sizefreq_2024.csv`. Adding these data would improve KFM urchin biomass estimation from cross-program PISCO bootstrap to within-program estimation. Deferred for the current manuscript and documented as a future-work item.

## 5. Combined size-frequency pool

- **File:** `data/ALL_sizefreq_2024.csv` (335,498 rows; 151 byte symlink)
- **Provenance:** Custom merge by Donham/Stier of size-frequency records from PISCO (UCSB + VRG), KFM, and LTER. Discriminated by the `method` column (`SBTL_SIZEFREQ_PISCO` / `SBTL_SIZEFREQ_VRG` / `SBTL_SIZEFREQ_KFM` / `SBTL_SIZEFREQ_LTER` / `SBTL_SWATH`).
- **Underlying citations:** Each contributing record cites its source program. PISCO via Malone et al. 2022, KFM via Davis et al. 1997, LTER via Reed & Miller (knb-lter-sbc.18, knb-lter-sbc.17).
- **Audit (2026-05-04):** For urchins (MESFRA + STRPUR), the pool contains 3,848 PISCO-UCSB + 16,810 PISCO-VRG + 0 KFM + 0 LTER records. For lobster (PANINT), 122 PISCO-UCSB + 1,940 PISCO-VRG + 2,109 SBTL_SWATH (UCSB direct measurement on swath). Effectively PISCO-only for both.
- **Schema:** `campus`, `method`, `survey_year`, `year`, `month`, `day`, `site_new`, `site`, `location`, `zone`, `transect`, `classcode`, `count`, `size`, `disease`, `depth`, `observer`, `notes`, `site_name_old`.
- **How we use it:** Read in `03_data_import.R` as `SizeFreq`, then filtered downstream for each program's biomass bootstrap.

## 7. Landsat-derived Macrocystis canopy biomass

- **File:** `data/LANDSAT/MPA_Runs_new.csv` (60 rows; 16,311 bytes)
- **Provenance:** Tom Bell (UCSB) polygon extraction of annual MAX kelp canopy biomass per MPA × status × replicate, derived from the SBC LTER Landsat kelp-watch product. Verified via three independent in-repo sources (Donham archive code, current pipeline header, DATA_DICTIONARY).
- **Underlying SBC LTER data package:**
  - **EDI package:** `knb-lter-sbc.74` (current revision 18+)
  - **EDI metadata:** https://portal.edirepository.org/nis/mapbrowse?packageid=knb-lter-sbc.74.18
  - **SBC catalog:** https://sbclter.msi.ucsb.edu/data/catalog/package/?package=knb-lter-sbc.74
  - The recovered `CAkelpCanopyEnv_2021.nc` records quarterly biomass in wet kg per 900 m^2 pixel. Quarterly pixel sums followed by annual maxima reproduce many delivered values, but not all values or missingness patterns. The precise extraction masks and coverage rules are not fully reconstructed; Kelpwatch is methodological context, not proof of the delivered extraction recipe.
- **Methodology citations:**
  > Cavanaugh, K.C., Siegel, D.A., Reed, D.C., & Dennison, P.E. (2011). Environmental controls of giant-kelp biomass in the Santa Barbara Channel, California. *Marine Ecology Progress Series* 429: 1–17. https://doi.org/10.3354/meps09141
  > Bell, T.W., Allen, J.G., Cavanaugh, K.C., & Siegel, D.A. (2020). Three decades of variability in California's giant kelp forests from the Landsat satellites. *Remote Sensing of Environment* 238: 110811. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2018.06.039
  > Bell, T.W., Cavanaugh, K.C., Saccomanno, V.R., Houskeeper, H.F., Eddy, N. et al. (2023). Kelpwatch: A new visualization and analysis tool to explore kelp canopy dynamics. *PLOS ONE* 18(3): e0271477. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0271477
- **Schema:** `MPA`, `status`, `rep`, `lat`, `lon`, then 38 year columns (1984–2021), each cell holding annual MAX canopy biomass (kg wet weight).
- **How we use it:** `06b_landsat_processing.R` removes only the six verified empty second-reference rows listed in `data/landsat_empty_reference_corrections.csv` before averaging. Observed zeros in occupied slots remain; years without a remaining reference observation are missing. The original 60-row CSV is preserved. `code/R/export_landsat_correction.R` regenerates the 718 paired MPA-years and 1,436 status-year annual indices, using the existing scaling and zero-offset calculation. Diver inputs are unchanged. Satellite canopy and diver-derived dry foliar biomass are distinct measurements; the synthesis combines source-specific contrasts, not interchangeable physical masses.

The correction is pinned by `output/harmonized/landsat_reference_correction.json` and the matching analysis-repository release marker. Rebuild from this repository with `Rscript code/R/export_landsat_correction.R /path/to/Donham-Stier-CA-MPA-2026`. The analysis repository then explicitly rebuilds its reference registry after refitting the primary histories.

## 8. Allometric biomass conversions (in `01_utils.R`)

The pipeline applies length-weight or count-stipe-to-biomass relationships in code (not data files), but each function has its own citation:

| Function | Used for | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `bio_lobster()` | *Panulirus interruptus* CL (mm) → biomass (g) | SBC LTER allometric calibration; cf. Barsky 2001, *CDFG Lobster Assessment.* |
| `bio_redurch()` | *Mesocentrotus franciscanus* test diameter (mm) → biomass (g) | SBC LTER allometric calibration; cf. Ebert 2010, *Sea Urchins: Biology and Ecology* (Academic Press). |
| `bio_purpurch()` | *Strongylocentrotus purpuratus* test diameter (mm) → biomass (g) | SBC LTER allometric calibration; cf. Ebert 2010. |
| `bio_sheephead()` / `apply_sheephead_allometry()` | *Semicossyphus pulcher* TL (cm) to individual mass (g); multiply by count before aggregation | Hamilton & Caselle (2015), Methods p. 2, DOI 10.1098/rspb.2014.1817; a = 0.0144, b = 3.04 in both PISCO and LTER. |
| `bio_macro(stipe, month)` | *Macrocystis pyrifera* stipe count or density → dry foliar biomass (g or g m^-2) | Survey-month slope from SBC 112.15 Table 5 model 1 times 1000; Rassweiler et al. (2026), DOI 10.6073/pasta/710844a10e2fe8873a4b49d97999d549. |

SBC `knb-lter-sbc.112.15` Table 5 was recovered through DataONE on 2026-10-12.
Its model 1 supplies twelve zero-intercept monthly slopes for total dry foliar
standing crop (0.07272-0.11996 kg dry mass per frond). It does not supply the
previous 0.08 constant or the previously used six-value mean. Month-specific
conversion now precedes annual aggregation; annual lnRRs were rebuilt rather
than assumed unchanged. The source snapshot and reproducible check are in the
analysis repository under `data/external/sbc11215/` and
`code/audit_kelp_calibration.py`. KFM resamples whole dated size records after
conversion, retaining site-year abundance matching; the surveys are not
necessarily month-matched. A single-record R sampling bug was corrected in
one unpaired 2007 density observation; no annual density ratio changed.
See the analysis repository's `docs/MONTHLY_KELP_REFIT_2026-10-12.md`.
